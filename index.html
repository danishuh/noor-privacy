Noor — Privacy Policy

Prayer Times & Qibla · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Noor is built to respect your privacy. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics tracking. The app works fully offline for its core features.

What information the app uses

  • Location (approximate or precise): Used only on your device to calculate accurate prayer times and the Qibla direction for where you are. You may also choose a city manually instead of using location.

How your location is used

To show official prayer times for your city, your coordinates may be sent securely (over HTTPS) to the public Aladhan prayer-times service to retrieve calculated times. This request contains only your coordinates — no name, email, device ID, or any personal identity. If you are offline, prayer times are calculated entirely on your device.

What we do NOT collect

  • No name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • No photos, files or messages.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app contains no ads SDK.
  • No account is created and nothing about you is stored on any server we control.

Notifications

If you enable the Azan or prayer reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No data leaves your phone for this.

Live streams

The “Makkah & Madinah Live” feature opens the official live streams on YouTube. Your use of YouTube is governed by Google/YouTube’s own privacy policy.

Children

Noor is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. Continued use of the app means you accept the current policy.
